## About us

Cedar Tech Solutions started with computer repair, fixing, upgrading and building machines for neighbours and small businesses, and grew into designing and running software of its own. We build products we would use ourselves, and we stay responsible for them after launch: hosting, support, privacy and updates.

The cedar in our name is the one on the Lebanese flag, a tree known for standing a long time. That is the standard we hold our work to, whether it is a PC we hand back or an app we publish: honest about what it does, and built to keep working.

## Our product: Radar

<a href="https://radar.cheap"><strong>Radar</strong></a> finds restaurant deals, daily specials and happy hours near you, with the hours each one actually runs.

Most deal listings go stale quietly. Radar stores the **schedule** behind each offer rather than a one-off coupon, so a happy hour that runs 3 to 6 pm Tuesday to Friday shows as on or off at the minute you ask. Every listing links to its source and says when it was last checked, and readers can flag anything that has changed.

- 🔎 **Free to browse.** No account needed to search, filter or use the map.
- 🕒 **Live availability.** See what is running right now, later today, or on a given day.
- ✅ **Transparent sourcing.** Each deal shows where it came from and how recently it was verified.
- 🔔 **Alerts with Radar Pro.** Track more areas, widen your radius and get notified about deals that match what you like.
- 🍽️ **For restaurants.** Owners can [list or update their deals](https://radar.cheap/en/for-restaurants) for free.
- 🌎 **Coverage.** Deepest in Ontario, with listings across Canada and nationwide US chains. Available on the web in English, French and Spanish, with an iPhone app on the way.
